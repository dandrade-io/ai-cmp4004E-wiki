# Lawrence J. Fogel

Creator of evolutionary computation (1960), using biological evolution principles to evolve **finite-state machines** (slide 2 of [[slides-04-optimization]]). His line of work is called **evolutionary programming** ([[evolutionary-programming]]): developed by Fogel et al. in the 1960s to simulate evolution as a learning process aimed at AI, with finite-state machines as individuals (Eiben & Smith p.103). Contemporaries: Rechenberg and Schwefel (evolution strategies, 1970) and [[john-holland]] (genetic algorithms, 1975).

See [[evolutionary-computation]], [[genetic-algorithms]].
