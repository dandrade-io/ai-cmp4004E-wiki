# Judea Pearl

Proposed the **Causal Calculus** (1987 per slide 15), encoding cause–effect relations in a Directed Acyclic Graph, presented as a promising bridge between ML and AI. See [[causal-models]]. Background (unverified): also known for Bayesian networks and the do-calculus; Turing Award 2011.

## From AIMA
The burglary/alarm/John/Mary network is due to Pearl (AIMA p. 431, [[bayesian-networks]]). AIMA's §13.5 presents his causal-network material (structural equations, do-operator, back-door criterion, p. 467–471), see [[causal-models]]; the do-calculus is described as a key part of the theory of causal networks. AIMA attributes the causal-analysis toolkit (interventions, counterfactuals, transportability, missing data) to work "in the past two decades" (p. 471). Pearl's own biography items (Turing Award 2011) remain (unverified) – not in AIMA text read.
