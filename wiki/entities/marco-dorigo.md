# Marco Dorigo

Proposed **Ant Colony Optimization** (slide 12 dates it 1996). The paper is [[paper-dorigo-ant-system-1996]]: Dorigo, Maniezzo & Colorni, "Ant System: Optimization by a Colony of Cooperating Agents", *IEEE Trans. SMC-B* 26(1), 1996. Co-authors Vittorio Maniezzo and Alberto Colorni (Politecnico di Milano); Dorigo was at Politecnico di Milano (AI & Robotics project) and by publication at IRIDIA, Université Libre de Bruxelles.

> ⚠️ Dating note: 1996 is the journal date; the manuscript was first received **Nov 1991**, and earlier versions appeared as tech reports/conferences in 1991–1992 (his PhD thesis 1992; refs [6],[7],[12],[13] in the paper). Slide 12's "Marco Dorigo – 1996" is therefore a publication date, not origin.
> Also: the paper names the method **Ant System**; "ACO" as the general metaheuristic name is later (unverified).
