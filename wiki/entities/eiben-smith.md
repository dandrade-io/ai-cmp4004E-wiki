# Eiben & Smith — *Introduction to Evolutionary Computing* (Springer, 2nd ed., 2015)

Textbook by A.E. (Gusz) Eiben and J.E. (Jim) Smith presenting EAs through the **generic EA scheme** and its components (representation, variation, selection), then variants as special cases (GA, ES, EP, GP, LCS, DE, PSO, EDA), methodology (parameters, control, experiments) and advanced topics. 2nd edition adds a chapter on problems and DE, PSO, EDA.

**Ingested chapters:** [[eiben-ch01-03-problems-origins-and-eas]], [[eiben-ch04-representation-variation]], [[eiben-ch05-selection-and-population-management]], [[eiben-ch06-popular-ea-variants]], [[eiben-ch07-09-parameters-control-and-experiments]] (partial), [[eiben-ch10-13-memetic-moo-constraints]] (partial), [[eiben-ch16-theory-no-free-lunch]] (§16.10 only). **Not ingested:** Ch.11 (dynamic/noisy), 14 (interactive EAs), 15 (coevolution), 17 (evolutionary robotics), most of Ch.16.

Companion general text: [[russell-norvig]]. Note the book has no chapter on [[ant-colony-optimization]] or [[artificial-bee-colony]].
