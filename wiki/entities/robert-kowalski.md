# Robert Kowalski

Logician at Edinburgh whose theory underpinned [[prolog]] (slide 8). Author of the slogan **"Algorithm = Logic + Control"** (1979, slide 2): the programmer supplies logic, the system supplies control. Foundation of [[logic-programming]], and of the execution model in [[sld-resolution-and-backtracking]].

Works with [[alain-colmerauer]] (implementation, Marseille 1972). Further background — SL/SLD resolution work — is not in the slides (unverified).

Exam angle: attribute the equation and place his role (theory) versus Colmerauer/Roussel (language).
