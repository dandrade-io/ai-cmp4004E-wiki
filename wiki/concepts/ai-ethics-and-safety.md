# AI Ethics and Safety – Overview

**Intuition.** AI is powerful, so builders have a moral (and in regulated domains legal) obligation to promote benefits and limit harms. Syllabus topic 10 ends with "ethics and safety" ([[slides-00-course-intro]]). Source: AIMA ch. 28 §28.3 (p. 1037 ff.).

## Benefits vs. risks (p. 1037–1038)
Benefits: medical diagnosis, disaster response, safer driving, accessibility, translation, near-zero marginal cost of software. Risks: unintended side effects of technology, wealth flowing to owners of automation (inequality), job disruption, misuse.

## Commonly cited principles (p. 1038)
Ensure safety · ensure fairness · respect privacy · promote collaboration · provide transparency · limit harmful uses · establish accountability · uphold human rights and values · reflect diversity/inclusion · avoid concentration of power · acknowledge legal/policy implications · contemplate implications for employment. Many are vaguely worded and hard to enforce; sub-fields should develop concrete guidelines and precedents.
