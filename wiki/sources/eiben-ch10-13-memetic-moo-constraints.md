# Eiben & Smith, Ch. 10, 12, 13 (pp. 167–214)

Book: [[eiben-smith]]. *Read in part: Ch.10 pp.167–171; Ch.12 pp.195–197; Ch.13 pp.203–208. Ch.11 (dynamic/noisy) and Ch.14–15 (interactive, coevolution) not ingested.*

- **Ch.10 Memetic algorithms**: EAs explore well but "fine-tune" poorly; add local search (hill climbers). Pivot rule (steepest vs greedy ascent), depth, neighbourhood; Lamarckian vs Baldwinian. → [[memetic-algorithms]].
- **Ch.12 Multiobjective**: dominance and the Pareto set/front. → [[multi-objective-optimization]].
- **Ch.13 Constraint handling**: indirect (penalties) vs direct (specialised representations/operators, repair, decoders). → [[constraint-handling-in-ea]].
