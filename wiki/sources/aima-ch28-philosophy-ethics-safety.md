## Highlights
- Twelve commonly cited AI principles (safety, fairness, privacy, transparency, accountability…).
- Six fairness definitions; COMPAS calibration vs equal-opportunity conflict; impossibility result of Kleinberg et al.
- Safety: FMEA, fault trees, low-impact agents, specification gaming, value alignment, IRL and assistance games.
