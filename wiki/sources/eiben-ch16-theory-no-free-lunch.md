# Eiben & Smith, Ch. 16 (pp. 231–244) — read: §16.10 and mentions

Book: [[eiben-smith]]. Only §16.10 (pp.243–244) and forward references (schema theorem §16.1, ES analysis) were read.

- **No Free Lunch (Wolpert & Macready)**: averaged over all problems, all non-revisiting black-box algorithms perform the same. Lessons: an algorithm great on one class pays elsewhere; problem-specific knowledge beats NFL → hybrids/[[memetic-algorithms]]. → [[no-free-lunch-theorem]].
- Rechenberg's analysis of the (1+1) ES on the sphere model yielded the **1/5 success rule** (p.243) → [[parameter-control]].
- The Schema Theorem (§16.1) is Holland's building-block argument (see [[paper-holland-genetic-algorithms]]); the book notes criticisms and that some experiments cast doubt on the building-block hypothesis (p.104).
